# Ajyra AI - ReproBridge v0.4 remaining limitations

- The scenario and rules are illustrative and clinically unapproved. The neutral metabolic assessment establishes neither a diagnosis nor any clinical benefit.
- The graph runs locally and deterministically. External LLM reasoning, external tools and outbound dispatch are future capabilities.
- The running indicator reflects an in-flight UI request. A persisted outcome appears on completion or failure. Earlier runs may lack a per-node trace.
- FHIR coverage is structural and bounded to patient-local reference checks. Full profile and terminology conformance and live EMR integrations remain future work.
- Postpartum actions and mother–child continuity are planned. Authentication remains shared demo-role access.
- Reset is restricted to the exact Amira fixture and refuses nonfixture evidence. Historical runs are archived, with summaries visible in-app.
- In-app evaluation counts are automated test results on synthetic cases. Clinical outcomes remain unmeasured.
