# Product scope

ReproBridge follows an outstanding care obligation when a synthetic patient moves from preconception into pregnancy. The three demo roles are coordinator, reviewer, and viewer. All rules and decisions in the prototype concern synthetic coordination records.

The demonstration covers life-stage transition intelligence, the Clinical Obligation Passport, LangGraph orchestration, cross-pathway dependencies, and evidence-verified closure. Mother–child continuity is a future extension with separate patient records and authorizations.

The population dashboard, women’s health workspace, obligation passport, care-resolution center, and evidence and audit view use the same persisted state. Wider life-stage coverage, clinical policy validation, and production deployment are outside this demonstration.
