# v0.6 increment limits

The implemented scope is local synthetic prevention tracking with an illustrative preconception metabolic prerequisite registry. Further clinical pathways need their own approved prerequisite definitions. Availability and document presence do not establish clinical sufficiency.

No approved clinical timing policy is included. Amira has no deadline. All configured policies are explicitly illustrative, and the separate timing scenario uses its first-load clock. Its deadlines age normally. Production timing rules require clinical governance and approved sources.

Authenticated reviewer identity is the shared demo reviewer role. Individual staff identity, consent and organizational authorization remain future integration work. The existing patient grants are enforced on decision and source reads.

Decision state and audits are persisted. An immutable recorded decision is not automatically retracted after source/policy changes. The view flags changed context so a clinician can determine whether another decision is needed. New reviews can be created through the decision API. Owner is assigned at creation, with reassignment UI deferred.

Gateway evaluation currently scans the local authorized patient catalog and synchronizes on serialized application writes and decision reads. Background scheduling, high-volume paging and large organizational queues remain future work. The current queue/history UI is intended for a small synthetic dataset.

Archived decision snapshots preserve source IDs, hashes, versions, citations and structured values. Original workflow reset archives retain the former clinical records. Some source navigation after reset uses the current M08 workflow scope.

The readiness agent preserves the six-node graph, provider bounds and seven tools. Its timing-advice screen is supplementary. Applied timing always comes from the deterministic policy engine. Narrative semantic interpretation remains a human responsibility, including packets whose source references and values validate.

The first real readiness packet was rejected safely for invalid citations and missing conflict acknowledgement. The successful subsequent run is retained for review. Model output is stochastic and each future packet is independently validated.

The local app has a separate illustrative timing patient and real readiness investigation/audit records. Original Amira clinical records were preserved. No clinical decision was automatically recorded, no real patient data was sent, and no model output was applied as a deadline.
